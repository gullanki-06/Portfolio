01 — About

Turning open questions into models that answer them.

I'm a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore. I'm drawn to research and development: taking an open question, framing it as a learning problem, and building a model that answers it.

Much of my work is aimed at disaster response: vision models that find survivors, swarm algorithms that plan rescue paths, and learning-based schedulers that keep emergency radio traffic flowing. I also work in NLP and computational biology, and my grounding in networking and cyber security shapes how I build systems that are reliable and safe.

03 — Selected work

Projects with real stakes.

01

Robotics · Computer Vision In progress

SwarmSight: Vision-Based Search & Rescue Robot

A rocker-bogie rover that searches disaster zones for survivors. A fine-tuned YOLO11n model detects people and individual body parts, so someone partly buried under rubble can still be found from a visible hand or leg. Runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 CPU via NCNN, avoids obstacles with ToF sensors and maps where people were detected.

Mission-Aware Continual Learning for SDR Emergency Networks

An AI packet scheduler for GNU Radio that ranks emergency traffic by a Dynamic Mission Criticality Score, so an SOS is never stuck behind telemetry. Learns online in the field, with a trust layer that stops spoofed nodes from faking priority.
